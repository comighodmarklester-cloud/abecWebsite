Request Guidelines

  • Select the document you need.
  • Choose the reason for your request.
  • Verify that all information is correct.
  • Wait for the administrator's review.
  • Track your request status in Request Tracking.
  • Check your notifications for updates and release instructions.

Important Reminder

Please request a document only if you genuinely need it and no longer have an existing copy. Make sure your reason is valid, as all requests will be reviewed by the ABEC Administration. Approval is subject to document request policies, including verification of your student status, payment records (if applicable), and eligibility for document release. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.
