What is ABEC Institute of Business and Technology Inc ?

ABEC Institute of Business and Technology, Inc. is a technical-vocational institution in Albay that provides quality skills training and career-focused education. ABEC helps learners develop practical competencies in business and technology through industry-relevant programs designed for future employment and professional growth.

Mission

To provide quality, accessible, and industry-relevant technical-vocational education and assessment services that equip individuals with the skills, knowledge, and confidence needed for professional success

Vision

To be a trusted and leading TESDA-accredited training and assessment center, recognized for developing highly skilled, competent, and globally competitive professionals.

Data Privacy Notice

This notice describes how the ABEC online enrollment and document release system handles personal information that you enter in this registration form. It is written for the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the general privacy-notice expectations of the National Privacy Commission. It describes this system as it currently works. Items the institution still has to confirm are marked as placeholders and are not presented as official policy.

Personal Information Collected

The registration form asks for information you provide, including:

  • Your name, suffix, gender, and civil status.
  • Your date of birth, age, and place of birth.
  • Your email address, mobile number, and any telephone, fax, or other contact number you enter.
  • Your education level and employment status.
  • Your address, including street, barangay, district, city or municipality, province, region, and ZIP code.
  • The course you are applying for, the qualification type, and your applicant classification.
  • The school, training center, or company name and address, when you provide them.
  • Your answers about previous, current, or completed TESDA-related training. These answers are used for ABEC’s initial screening. This system does not treat them as an official TESDA eligibility decision.
  • The type of valid ID you select, and the ID file you upload.
  • Credential files you upload, such as a transcript of records, Form 138, honorable dismissal, certificate of good moral character, or Birth Certificate (PSA).

After you submit, the system also creates an application reference and keeps the application status. If a message is sent, it records whether that email was sent or failed.

Purpose of Data Collection and Processing

The system uses this information to:

  • Receive and review your enrollment application.
  • Show the application to authorized ABEC administrators, including the course and uploaded files.
  • Support the administrator’s initial check of the TESDA-related answers you entered.
  • Send a confirmation or later status message to the email address you provided. The confirmation can include a PDF copy of your application form.
  • Tell you, in that message, to print the form and bring it to the ABEC office when asked.
  • Notify administrators that a new application was submitted.
  • Keep the same application available if ABEC later confirms a slot and continues enrollment or document release in this system.

The form also states that the details you enter are used as the basis for reporting and for printing the corresponding certificate.

Legal Basis for Processing

The information needed to receive and administer your application is processed because you asked ABEC to take steps toward enrollment. Where a separate legal obligation applies to a TESDA-registered training institution, that obligation has to be confirmed by the institution. This notice does not create one.

Checking the acknowledgment box only confirms that you have read and understood this notice. It is not consent to every processing activity. If a particular use depends on consent under Republic Act No. 10173, that consent has to be requested on its own and must not be assumed from this checkbox.

Data Storage and Security

Your application details are stored in this system’s database. Uploaded credential and ID files are stored on the server outside the public website folder, under the application’s secure upload area. Authorized administrator accounts can open those records inside the system.

This notice does not claim a specific security certification, encryption standard, or National Privacy Commission registration. Those items are still for the institution to confirm.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Within this system, your application and files are available to authorized ABEC administrators. Status messages, including a PDF of the application form when it is attached, are sent to your own email address through the email service configured for this system.

Submitting the form does not, by itself, transmit your record to TESDA or another government agency. The software has no automatic transfer of that kind. If ABEC later shares information outside this system, including for TESDA training or because a law requires it, that sharing is an institutional practice and is not performed automatically here.

Other recipients — to be confirmed by the institution Any regular disclosure to TESDA, a scholarship office, an insurer, or another organization is not defined in this system and must be added by ABEC before it is treated as part of this notice.

Data Retention Period

This system does not delete applications on a timer. A record and its uploaded files stay until an authorized administrator changes or removes them.

Retention period — to be confirmed by the institution The length of time ABEC must keep enrollment, assessment, and certificate records is not set in this software. ABEC needs to state that period after checking its TESDA and institutional requirements.

Rights of Data Subjects

Under Republic Act No. 10173, you have rights that include the right to be informed, to access your personal data, to correct it, to object to or suspend certain processing, to request erasure or blocking where the law allows it, to data portability where it applies, and to claim damages where the law provides a remedy. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

How ABEC will receive and act on those requests depends on the designated privacy contact below. That contact is not yet identified in this system.
